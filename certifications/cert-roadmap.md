# SOC Certification Roadmap

## Overview
Structured certification pathway for SOC analysts at each career stage. Certifications are grouped by role level and focus area. Study resources and estimated timelines are included for planning purposes.

---

## Tier 1 — SOC Analyst (L1/L2)

### Foundational (Complete within first 12 months)

| Certification | Body | Focus | Study Time | Priority |
|--------------|------|-------|-----------|---------|
| CompTIA Security+ | CompTIA | Security fundamentals, threats, cryptography | 60–80 hrs | **Must-have** |
| CompTIA CySA+ | CompTIA | Threat detection, SIEM, incident response | 80–100 hrs | **Must-have** |
| SC-200: Microsoft Security Operations Analyst | Microsoft | Sentinel, Defender, KQL | 40–60 hrs | High |
| Google Cybersecurity Certificate | Google / Coursera | SOC fundamentals, Linux, Python | 20–30 hrs | Good starter |

### Vendor / Tool-Specific (As applicable to your environment)

| Certification | Vendor | Relevance |
|--------------|--------|----------|
| Splunk Core Certified User | Splunk | SIEM querying |
| Splunk Core Certified Power User | Splunk | Advanced SPL |
| CrowdStrike Certified Falcon Responder | CrowdStrike | EDR triage |
| Palo Alto PCNSA | Palo Alto | Firewall and network security |

## Tier 2 — Senior SOC Analyst / IR Analyst

### Intermediate (Target: 1–3 years experience)

| Certification | Body | Focus | Study Time | Priority |
|--------------|------|-------|-----------|---------|
| GIAC Security Essentials (GSEC) | SANS/GIAC | Broad security operations | 100–150 hrs | High |
| GIAC Certified Incident Handler (GCIH) | SANS/GIAC | Incident response, forensics | 100–150 hrs | **Core IR cert** |
| GIAC Certified Enterprise Defender (GCED) | SANS/GIAC | Defence architecture | 120–160 hrs | High |
| EC-Council CEH | EC-Council | Ethical hacking, attacker mindset | 80–100 hrs | Useful |
| eLearnSecurity eJPT | eLearnSecurity | Practical penetration testing basics | 40–60 hrs | Good hands-on |

## Tier 3 — SOC Lead / Threat Hunter / IR Specialist

### Advanced (Target: 3+ years experience)

| Certification | Body | Focus | Study Time | Priority |
|--------------|------|-------|-----------|---------|
| GIAC Certified Forensic Analyst (GCFA) | SANS/GIAC | Memory forensics, intrusion analysis | 150–200 hrs | **Core forensics** |
| GIAC Certified Forensic Examiner (GCFE) | SANS/GIAC | Windows forensics, disk analysis | 150–200 hrs | High |
| GIAC Network Forensic Analyst (GNFA) | SANS/GIAC | Network traffic analysis | 120–150 hrs | High |
| OSCP (Offensive Security Certified Professional) | Offensive Security | Penetration testing (red team context) | 200+ hrs | High value |
| GIAC Threat Intelligence (GCTI) | SANS/GIAC | CTI analysis and production | 100–130 hrs | High |

## Tier 4 — SOC Manager

### Management & Strategy (Target: 5+ years experience)

| Certification | Body | Focus | Study Time | Priority |
|--------------|------|-------|-----------|---------|
| CISSP | (ISC)² | Security management, architecture | 200–300 hrs | **Must-have for manager** |
| CISM | ISACA | Information security management | 150–200 hrs | **Strong alternative to CISSP** |
| CISA | ISACA | Audit and assurance | 150–200 hrs | High — compliance focus |
| ISO 27001 Lead Implementer | PECB / BSI | ISMS implementation | 40–60 hrs | Valuable |
| ISO 27001 Lead Auditor | PECB / BSI | ISMS auditing | 40–60 hrs | Valuable |
| SANS MGT551 | SANS | Building and leading SOC teams | 40 hrs | Highly recommended |

## Budget & Study Time Policy

- Each analyst is allocated a **training budget** — confirm amount with SOC Manager
- Study time: up to **2 hours per week** of dedicated study during work hours (approved by manager)
- Exam fees covered by the team training budget upon manager approval
- Exam retake (first retake only) covered if the analyst completes all recommended study materials
